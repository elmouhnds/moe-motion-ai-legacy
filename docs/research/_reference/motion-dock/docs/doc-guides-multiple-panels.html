Use multiple dock panels

v13

Open up to five independent dock canvases and place them around your After Effects workspace.

Use another panel when you want two arrangements visible at the same time. For example, keep a narrow toolbar beside the timeline and a larger dock for the tools you are working with.

Open another panel

  1. Click Settings, the gear in the dock’s top bar.
  2. Select Motion Dock in the settings sidebar.
  3. Under Panels, find Open Another Motion Dock Panel.
  4. Click Motion Dock 2, Motion Dock 3, Motion Dock 4 or Motion Dock 5.
  5. Dock or float the new panel in After Effects, resize it, then choose a starter or add tools.

The current panel’s button is disabled. Opening an existing panel brings up that panel; it does not create another copy of its canvas.

A second Motion Dock panel opens and is docked into the After Effects workspace.

Whether it’s isolated.

Changing a shared tool setting in one panel can affect the same tool elsewhere. Separate panels give you separate canvases, rather than separate installations of the tools.

There are five dock panels in total, including the first Motion Dock panel. Each one can show one of its three layouts at a time.
