Meet Motion Dock

v13

Get to know the panel that brings your Motion Fun tools together in After Effects.

Motion Dock puts Motion Fun’s tools in a panel you arrange yourself, so the things you reach for can stay close to your work.

A few tools can share one panel. Give each a slot, and keep the ones you use in view.

Several tools running from Motion Dock in After Effects.

Start with a layout

An empty dock offers four starters. Choose one to get a ready-made arrangement, or choose Blank to start with an empty grid. You can change either one later.

The first layout guide takes you from an open dock to a working tool in a few steps.

Make room for what you use

Open Edit layout to add tools, move them or resize them. A tool can sit in a wide row, share a row with another tool or have more space of its own. Its interface adapts to the space it has.

You choose which tools belong in your layout. Leave the rest in the picker until you need them. Arrange your tools when you are ready to make the dock yours.

Keep a few ways to work

Each dock panel has three layouts. Use one for animation, another for organizing layers and another for finishing a project. Switch with the numbered buttons or 1, 2 and 3 while the dock has focus.

Need tools visible in more than one place? You can open another dock panel and place it elsewhere in your After Effects workspace.

The dock and its tools

The dock takes care of the workspace. Each tool takes care of a particular task, with its own controls and documentation.
